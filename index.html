About

I am a biologist working at the intersection of microbial ecology, microbiology, genomics, extremophile biology, symbiosis, and computational biology.

My research examines how organisms survive, interact, and adapt under challenging environmental conditions, spanning microbial systems, host-associated symbioses, extreme terrestrial environments, and space biology.

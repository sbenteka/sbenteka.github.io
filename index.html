About

I am a biologist working at the intersection of microbial ecology, genomics, extremophile biology, symbiosis, and computational biology. My research focuses on understanding how organisms survive, interact, and adapt under challenging environmental conditions.

Research

Extremophile Microbiology

Investigating microorganisms adapted to extreme environments, with a focus on taxonomy, physiology, genomics, stress tolerance, and ecological function.

Space Biology

Studying how organisms respond and adapt to spaceflight and space-associated stress conditions.

Microbial Symbiosis

Exploring host-associated microbial systems, including Frankia-actinorhizal symbioses and mechanisms shaping microbial abundance, host selection, and adaptation.

Bioinformatics & Data Science

Applying RNA-seq, genome analysis, statistical modeling, visualization, and computational workflows to biological questions across microbial and stress-biology systems.

Selected Work

Space Biology

Research examining biological responses to spaceflight and space-associated environmental stress.

Wyoming Red Desert Microbiology

Genomic, physiological, and taxonomic characterization of novel bacterial isolates recovered from extreme rock-surface environments.

Frankia Symbiosis

Research on the ecology, abundance, host selection, and molecular biology of nitrogen-fixing Frankia associated with actinorhizal plants.
